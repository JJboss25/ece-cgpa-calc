CGPA = total quality points ÷ total units of graded courses. Quality points for a course = units × grade points. Courses you leave ungraded are ignored.

Carried over courses: tick "Carried over" on a course you failed and are retaking. The earlier sitting is treated as an F (0 quality points), so the course's quality points become (units × new grade points) ÷ 2 — the retake averaged with that 0. Units still count once toward the total.
